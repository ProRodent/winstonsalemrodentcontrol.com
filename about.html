Who We Are

A Forsyth County-Rooted Rodent Specialist

Winston-Salem Rodent Control is a locally-owned rat and mouse removal company running out of 1847 Reynolda Road in West Winston-Salem. We focus on what the name says, rodent work. Not termites, not mosquitoes, not the full general-pest portfolio. Just rats, mice, and the exclusion, sanitation, and prevention services that really resolve those infestations.

We started this business after years of watching homeowners in Ardmore, the West End. And Reynolda Park cycle through national pest-control chains that signed them up for quarterly contracts. Sprayed something around the foundation, and never really closed the entry points. Rodent control isn't a recurring spray problem. It's an exclusion problem layered with a trapping problem, and most companies in this region don't have the patience or specialization to do it right.

So we built the company to do it the other way around. Free inspection first. Written quote second. And a single thorough job. Trapping plus actual physical sealing. That resolves the problem rather than masking it.

What Makes Winston-Salem Different

Every market has its own rodent profile. Winston-Salem's has three distinct shapes:

  • Norway rats work the older system, Old Salem's brick-pier foundations, the sewer-adjacent lots in West Salem and Southside, and the basement-equipped masonry housing near the Reynolds Building. They surge in late winter when sewer-line populations push outward looking for new territory.
  • Roof rats dominate the Reynolda canopy belt. Mature hardwoods stretching from Reynolda Gardens through Buena Vista, Mount Tabor, and Forest Hills give them tree-access highways into soffit gaps, gable vents, and dormer-roof penetrations on the larger older homes.
  • House mice are everywhere, but concentrate hardest in the pre-1970s housing stock across Ardmore, Holly Avenue, Washington Park, and Boston Thurmond, bungalows, shotgun cottages, and mill-village houses with eighty years of settling foundations and original-construction gaps around plumbing penetrations.

Knowing which of those three you really have, and which combination, is the entire job. A roof-rat treatment plan applied to a Norway-rat problem will fail. A mouse-baiting protocol applied to a roof-rat ceiling-void infestation will fail. We do the inspection because guessing wrong is costly for you and embarrassing for us.

How We Work

Our work is the same on every job, residential or commercial:

  1. Free inspection. We walk the property, interior, attic or crawl space, exterior perimeter, plumbing penetrations, soffit and roofline. You're welcome to walk with us. We don't quote until we know what we're looking at.
  2. Written quote. Itemized. What's included, what's not, what the prevention plan looks like, and what the range of likely outcomes is. No verbal estimates that change at the end.
  3. Execution. Species-right treatment, snap-trap arrays in protected runways, tamper-resistant exterior bait stations, attic exclusion materials, foundation sealing, droppings cleanup and sanitation where needed.
  4. Follow-up. Most active infestations need at least one return visit to check knockdown and reset traps. That's included on every job at no extra charge.

Licensing and Rule-following

North Carolina structural pest control regulations apply to all chemical treatments. We run inside those rules. Where we use rodenticide, it's placed in compliant tamper-resistant bait stations, placed and labeled per state structural pest control board needs. Mechanical exclusion and trapping, which makes up most of what we do, falls under standard contractor work.

We carry general liability insurance. We don't claim certifications we don't have, awards we haven't earned, or "trusted by thousands" numbers we can't substantiate. What you see on the site is what we really do, no inflated review counts, no fabricated star ratings, no manufactured social proof. Trust gets built over time through the work itself.
