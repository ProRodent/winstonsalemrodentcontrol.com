What Happens on the Call

  1. Two minutes of triage. We ask what you're seeing, droppings, scratching, dead-rodent odor, sightings, area of the house. That tells us whether you're dealing with mice, Norway rats, or roof rats.
  2. Scheduling. If it's an active infestation called in before mid-afternoon, we look at same-day across Forsyth County. Otherwise we set the next-available inspection slot, usually within 24-48 hours.
  3. On-site inspection. Free. Walk-through of the interior, attic or crawl space, exterior perimeter, and entry-point map. You're welcome to walk with us.
  4. Written quote. Itemized scope, prevention plan, and a clear range. No verbal estimates that grow at the end. No contracts.

No high-pressure sales. No quarterly auto-renew traps. Just inspection, quote, and execution.
